# Unofficial Gaelic Companion

An independent companion to "Scottish Gaelic in Twelve Weeks" for iPhone and
iPad. Import audio you are entitled to use, browse lessons and sections, track
listening progress, and listen with background and lock-screen controls.
The app is free, contains no publisher recordings, and has no ads or accounts.
It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the book's authors or publisher.

## Getting started

1. Open the publisher's download page using the link in the app.
2. Download the original ZIP and save it in Files without extracting it.
3. Return to the app and choose **Import downloaded ZIP**.
4. Open a lesson and select a recording. The source ZIP is not modified.

## Common questions

**The ZIP will not import.** Keep the original ZIP rather than a browser-extracted
folder. The app expects individual MP3s with the publisher's numeric track IDs
and title metadata. An incomplete or unsupported archive produces a visible
error and does not replace an existing library.

**There is no sound.** Check playback volume and the current audio output device.
Choose a recording before pressing Play.

**How do I replace audio?** Use **About & download -> Replace imported audio**.
A successful replacement preserves listened status. A failed or cancelled
replacement leaves the existing library available.

**How do I remove my data?** Reset listened status in **About & download**.
Deleting the app (not offloading it) removes its local library and preferences.
Your source ZIP and device backups are managed separately.

**Is the Mac editor the same product?** No. It is an optional source-repository
tool for maintaining intro markers, not required for the iPhone/iPad app.
